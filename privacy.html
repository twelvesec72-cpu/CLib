GoughRead Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

GoughRead is a catalogue for your own physical book collection. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics, and the developer runs no server that receives your data.

What stays on your device

Your books, collections, notes, settings and any cover photos you take are stored only on your phone, inside the app's private storage. Nobody else, including the developer, can see them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Camera

The app asks for the camera to scan book barcodes and, if you choose, to photograph covers. Barcode frames are read on the device and thrown away; cover photos are saved on the device only. Nothing from the camera is uploaded anywhere.

Book lookups

When you scan or search for a book, the app sends the ISBN or your search words to Open Library (run by the Internet Archive) to fetch the title, author and cover. That request contains no personal information and is not linked to you by GoughRead. Open Library's own terms and privacy policy apply to it.

These lookups are your in-app search history. GoughRead sends each one only to Open Library, only to answer that request, and keeps no record of your searches. The one thing that stays is a book you choose to add, which is saved on your phone with the rest of your library.

Backups

You can export your library as a file. Where that file goes is up to you: the app hands it to Android's share sheet, and you choose the destination, such as Google Drive, email, or your phone's files. GoughRead does not send it anywhere by itself.

If Android's own device backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's data in that backup. That is controlled by Android and your Google account settings, not by GoughRead.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
